I launch small, read the signal, then scale

Educato isn't betting on one giant account. It launches several small ones, market by market. That is exactly how I work: I run small topic pages in adjacent niches to test a format before committing to it. Most don't survive, and that is the point. The signal tells me where demand already is, so the next test aims better.

  1. Launch small

    Start a test account in the niche where the audience already searches.

  2. Read the signal

    Watch early retention, shares, and follows, not vanity counts.

  3. Pause what flops

    Kill a failing format fast instead of defending it.

  4. Double down on what works

    Point budget and effort at the formats the market voted for.

Proof of method: multiple topic pages run as tests across niches, each logged with a go or no-go verdict.

The first 30 days I'd spend in your market

If I owned the UGC program for a market, here is the sequence I'd run. Judge me on the sequence, not on a promise.

  1. Map the market

    List the creators who already move students in your market, by subject and platform.

  2. Build a roster

    Shortlist 10 to 20 local creators across follower bands, so the mix is testable.

  3. Negotiate and onboard

    One standard brief, clear rates, and fixed deliverables from day one.

  4. Brief with proven formats

    Reuse the formats that already work in that market rather than inventing new ones.

  5. Review every day

    Fast, specific feedback on first drafts so creators never wait on me.

  6. Keep the boring part boring

    Deadlines, deliverables, and payments tracked tightly so creators keep producing.
